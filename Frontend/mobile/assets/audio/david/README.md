These are original, procedurally synthesized sounds for David's Sling Aim.
No recordings, cloned voices, or external sound libraries were used.

- `battle_ambience.wav`: 24-second stereo loop, 44.1 kHz PCM. Periodic filtered
  wind, distant hand drums, two low horn calls, and occasional cloth shuffles.
  Its synthesis wraps naturally across the loop boundary.
- `sling_launch.wav`: 550 ms pouch snap and airy stone launch.
- `stone_impact.wav`: 340 ms dry stone contact.
- `body_land.wav`: 820 ms low ground thud and soft cloth/sand settling.

All files are 16-bit PCM with headroom and no clipped samples. The ambience
and effects have separate playback levels. There are no human voices,
fighting screams, injury sounds, or gore cues.
